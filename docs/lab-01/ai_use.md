## Reflection

I improved my prompts by specifying the exact Issue number, required format, and the part of the project I was working on. This made the AI responses more focused and easier to apply to the TokTickIT project. I also checked the generated suggestions against the Lab requirements and corrected or rejected suggestions when they included work that belonged to another Issue or did not match the required workflow.
